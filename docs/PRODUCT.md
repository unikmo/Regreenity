# Cruise Connection — MVP Product Definition

## Positioning

Cruise Connection is a white-label add-to-app module that turns onboard human interaction into crew recognition, immediate service recovery, greater participation, incremental commerce and shareable positive memories.

It is not a conventional social network. The ship is the social environment. Cruise Connection creates safe first contact and reasons to participate together; it does not host unrestricted ongoing conversation.

## Core flows

### Crew recognition
1. Guest opens recognition flow.
2. Sailing, ship, time and available venue context are known.
3. Guest photographs the visible employee badge / identifier.
4. Guest selects a predefined positive recognition reason.
5. Recognition is attributed to the employee and sailing context.
6. Crew member and cruise line can receive structured summaries.

### Private service recovery
1. Guest chooses Need help.
2. Guest selects a predefined issue category.
3. Issue is routed privately to the relevant operating team.
4. Cruise line acknowledges, assigns, resolves and closes the loop.
5. The issue never becomes a public rating inside Cruise Connection.

### Passenger affirmations and invitations
1. Only verified passengers on the same sailing participate.
2. Guest chooses a predefined positive affirmation or activity invitation.
3. Recipient can acknowledge, ignore, block or report.
4. No unrestricted chat is provided.
5. Guests who wish to continue communicating can meet in person, exchange contact details, or use the cruise line's own messaging environment.

### Participation and commerce
1. Positive passenger interaction creates group context.
2. Shared interest is detected around an onboard experience.
3. Relevant availability can be surfaced at an appropriate moment.
4. Booking remains inside the cruise line's own commerce stack.
5. Cruise Connection measures agreed attribution / incrementality.

### End-of-cruise summaries
Participating passengers receive a personal collection of positive affirmations. Crew can receive a recognition summary. Public sharing should expose the recipient's summary, not private sender identities without explicit consent.
