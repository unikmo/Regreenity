Why this matters

FAQ

Common questions

Commercial pilots should be explicit about scope, integration and the outcome being measured.

Does Regreenity replace a PMS?

No. Regreenity is an engagement layer. PMS, CRS, POS and other systems remain systems of record.

Can a resort use the same platform as a city hotel?

Yes. Resorts may use more participation and activity workflows; hotels may focus more on recovery, recognition and relevant ancillary offers.

Can Regreenity work without a native hotel app?

Yes. The architecture can support embedded web experiences as well as host-app integration.
