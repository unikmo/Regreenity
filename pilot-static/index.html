How it works

Guest signal → operational action → measurable outcome

The same signal should not go to every team. Regreenity gives each interaction an intentional route.

01 · Signal

Capture the moment

A guest joins, recognizes, rates, checks in, reports an issue or expresses an interest.

02 · Action

Route it correctly

The platform sends the signal to engagement, service recovery, recognition or a relevant commercial workflow.

03 · Outcome

Measure what changed

Participation, recovery, follow-up and attribution can be measured at property or sailing level.

Architecture

Not another standalone guest app

Regreenity is designed to fit the operator’s existing digital relationship. The cruise line, hotel or resort keeps identity, inventory, pricing, payments and the core guest experience.
