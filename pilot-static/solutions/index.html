Why this matters

Engagement only creates value when somebody can act on it.

Tisonik is designed around the operating moment: what the guest is trying to do, what the team needs to know, and what the next useful action should be.

The objective is not more messages. It is a better connection between guest intent, service action and measurable outcome.

FAQ

Common questions

Commercial pilots should be explicit about scope, integration and the outcome being measured.

Do we need every solution at launch?

No. Tisonik is intended to support modular pilots.

Are cruise and hotel workflows identical?

The operating model is shared, while terminology, roles and integrations adapt to the vertical.

Can the same platform support both a cruise group and hotel group?

Yes at platform level, while customer environments remain separately configured and governed.
