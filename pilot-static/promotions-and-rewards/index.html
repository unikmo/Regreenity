FAQ

Common questions

Commercial pilots should be explicit about scope, integration and the outcome being measured.

Can Marketing and Revenue have separate roles?

Yes. The cruise pilot includes separate Marketing and Sales/Revenue staff roles.

Can promotions include alcohol?

Prize and promotion configuration should respect age and operator policy; the pilot supports alternate non-alcoholic prize treatment.

Does Regreenity provide loyalty points?

Regreenity can integrate with a loyalty program, but it is not intended to replace the operator’s loyalty ledger.
