Why this matters

Engagement only creates value when somebody can act on it.

Regreenity is designed around the operating moment: what the guest is trying to do, what the team needs to know, and what the next useful action should be.

The objective is not more messages. It is a better connection between guest intent, service action and measurable outcome.

Outcomes

What changes operationally

Regreenity links guest-facing interactions to operational and commercial follow-through, rather than treating engagement as an isolated front-end feature.

Reuse one engine

Support check-ins, polls, trivia, flash draws and other participation formats with a shared campaign lifecycle.

Keep eligibility controlled

Use verified guest context and approved targeting rather than anonymous public entries.

Measure live adoption

Give staff live counts and status controls during the activity.

Workflow

From signal to action

The platform is deliberately structured so each interaction has a clear state, owner and next step.

Create

Staff creates a draft campaign with timing, type, eligibility and prize rules if applicable.

Go live

Guests see and complete the relevant participation action.

Close and measure

End entries, publish results or run a secure server-side draw when the campaign type requires it.

FAQ

Common questions

Commercial pilots should be explicit about scope, integration and the outcome being measured.

Can one guest enter multiple times?

The current pilot enforces one verified passenger entry per campaign. Production rules can be adapted by campaign type.

Does participation require a purchase?

No purchase is required by default.

Can campaigns be scheduled?

The campaign lifecycle supports draft, scheduled, live, closed and completed/drawn states.
