Why this matters

Engagement only creates value when somebody can act on it.

Regreenity is designed around the operating moment: what the guest is trying to do, what the team needs to know, and what the next useful action should be.

The objective is not more messages. It is a better connection between guest intent, service action and measurable outcome.

FAQ

Common questions

Commercial pilots should be explicit about scope, integration and the outcome being measured.

Does Regreenity replace NPS?

Not necessarily. Regreenity can complement broader survey programs with in-experience operational signals.

Can guests submit text?

Text can be enabled where needed, but the core philosophy is to use structured responses whenever possible.

Can feedback be department-specific?

Yes. The cruise pilot includes department-level pulse concepts, and hotel/resort deployment can map to property departments.
