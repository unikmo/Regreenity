Cookie & Local Storage Policy

What the current Tisonik website and pilot store on your device.

Last updated 18 August 2026

1. Current public website

The current Tisonik marketing website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or non-essential analytics cookies. It uses ordinary browser functionality required to render pages and submit the pilot-request form.

2. Pilot simulator

The protected pilot simulator may use session storage, Local Storage, Cache Storage and a service worker to preserve pilot authentication state, demonstrate offline queues and cache the application shell. These are functional technologies rather than advertising trackers.

3. Customer deployments

A cruise line, hotel or resort may use its own authentication, analytics or app-storage technologies around an embedded Tisonik module. The customer’s privacy and cookie information applies to customer-controlled technologies.

4. Managing storage

You can delete browser storage through your browser or device settings. Clearing essential pilot storage may sign you out, remove queued demonstration actions or require cached files to be downloaded again.

5. Changes

If Tisonik later adds non-essential analytics or marketing technologies, we will update this page and introduce any consent mechanism required by applicable law before activating them.
