Why this matters

Engagement only creates value when somebody can act on it.

Tisonik is designed around the operating moment: what the guest is trying to do, what the team needs to know, and what the next useful action should be.

The objective is not more messages. It is a better connection between guest intent, service action and measurable outcome.

FAQ

Common questions

Commercial pilots should be explicit about scope, integration and the outcome being measured.

Does Tisonik set cruise prices?

No. Inventory, pricing, payments, refunds and fulfilment stay with the cruise line.

Is this only upselling?

No. Revenue is one outcome of a broader engagement platform. The same guest signal may be better used for participation or service recovery.

Can sales teams create promotions?

The pilot includes Marketing and Sales/Revenue roles for controlled promotional workflows.
